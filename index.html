FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with businesses that already have an existing PHP website or system?

Yes — this is a large part of what I do. I can review existing PHP/MySQL code, diagnose issues and modify or extend functionality rather than rebuilding from zero.

Can you customize our existing ERP instead of building a new one?

In most cases, yes. I'll first look at how your current system works before recommending whether customization or a new module makes more sense.

Do you work directly with businesses, or only through agencies?

Both. I take on direct client projects, and I also work as a development partner behind agencies on a project or white-label basis.

What if I don't know the exact scope of my project yet?

That's normal. Share what you're trying to achieve and I'll help scope it out during the requirements discussion, before any coding starts.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance and support?

Yes, for both projects I've built and existing PHP/MySQL systems I'm brought in to maintain.

Can you work with my existing hosting (cPanel / shared hosting)?

Yes, I regularly deploy and troubleshoot on standard shared hosting and cPanel environments common with Indian businesses.
